# Pitchkit trust test: script (draft, nobody contacted)

**Goal:** check that the trust layer makes Pitchkit's Instagram numbers more believable to brands than a Beacons-style kit.
**Who:** 5 brand or agency marketers who hire Instagram creators. Randy approves recruiting and any outreach.
**Materials:** side-by-side of Kit A and Kit B at phone size, from Product Designer, in this folder. Both show EXAMPLE DATA, carry no brand names, and appear in random left/right order.

## Questions (about 5 minutes per marketer)
1. "You're deciding whether to hire this creator. Look at both kits for 60 seconds."
2. "Which kit's numbers do you trust more?" (A, B or same) "Why?"
3. "What, if anything, would you check somewhere else before hiring?"
4. "Rate how believable each kit's numbers are, from 1 to 5."
5. "Is anything missing that you'd need before deciding?"

## Pass line
At least 4 of 5 pick Kit A as more trustworthy. Fewer than 4 means the trust layer isn't the first thing to build, and we rethink.

## Record
Pick, reason, believability scores, outside checks named (Modash, HypeAuditor, a screenshot request) and what's missing.
